A. OPERATOR & GATEKEEPER NAVIGATION (14 SCENARIOS)
- General Operator Pitch:
  "Good morning, I am trying to reach the person who takes care of the sales of the surplus equipment such as: forklifts and wheel loaders?"

- If asked "What is this in regards to?":
  "This is [Name] with JYC Equipment. I'm calling because we buy used heavy equipment, such as forklifts and wheel loaders. I wanted to check if you might have anything for sale right now or maybe coming up this year? Do you know who would be the person I need to talk to about it?"

- Operator Responses & Exact Rebuttals:
  1. "No idea":
     "Usually the Operations Manager or a Fleet Manager would know. Or somebody in maintenance?"
  2. "The person is not available at this time":
     - "May I have his name please?"
     - "Does he have an email address where I can send him my contact information?"
     - "What would be the best number to reach him at?"
  3. "All our equipment is new":
     "When the time comes, do you know who would handle that type of sales in the future?"
  4. "We don't have any at this time":
     "Could you please tell me who would be the person I need to contact in the future?"
  5. "We don't have any (don't use)":
     "Do you use forklifts or wheel loaders in operations? Could you please tell me who would be the person I need to contact in the future?"
  6. "We don't sell our equipment":
     "What do you usually do when the company no longer has a need of them? Do you trade them in or lease them?"
  7. "Our equipment is very old":
     "We buy equipment in any condition. We just need to know what's wrong with them. Could you please tell me who would be the person I need to contact in the future?"
  8. "We don't do that":
     "Ok, I understand you don't sell used equipment, however your company uses forklifts or wheel loaders in daily operations. When this equipment becomes surplus we could pay more than any other company since we export. Could you please tell me who is the person I need to contact?"
  9. "We are not interested":
     "We are not selling you anything, we are major buyers of used equipment, and we pay more than any other company since we export. I'm sure the person who handles this will be very interested since your company will be getting more money for the equipment. Could you please tell me who is the person I need to contact?"
  10. "How do you work since Miami?":
     "We buy used equipment all over the United States and Canada. We just need some information and pictures on the equipment that you have and if we get to a price agreement, we will wire you the money within 24 hours. Once you have the money in your bank, we will pickup the equipment."
  11. "We lease our equipment":
     "We also buy end-of-lease equipment. Do you know who handles those leasing contracts?"
  12. "We rent our equipment":
     "By rent do you mean you lease them or you rent them for a few days?"
  13. "We trade in our equipment":
     "We could pay you more than the trade-in value of the machines since we export. Could you please tell me who is the person I need to talk to?" (Note: Matrix Plastics handles direct disposition and does not use trade-in).
  14. "We auction our equipment":
     "Do you know who I can talk to about the equipment that the company would auction soon?"
