4. COLD CALL SCRIPT FLOW (TRAINEE VS PROSPECT)
================================================================================

A. GETTING PAST THE OPERATOR (GATEKEEPER BYPASS)
- Receptionist Intro (Trainee):
  "Good morning, could you please transfer me to the person in charge of plant operations or the used machinery, like your injection molding machines, forklifts, or loaders?"
  (En español: "Buenos días, ¿me podría comunicar con el encargado de planta, operaciones o la maquinaria usada como sus inyectoras de plástico, montacargas o cargadores?")
- Operator (You) asks: "What is this in regards to?" / "¿De parte de quién y sobre qué asunto?"
- Trainee explains:
  "This is [Name] with JYC Equipment. We are nationwide buyers of used heavy machinery and equipment. I wanted to check with the plant manager if you might have any surplus machines for sale or coming up for renewal this year. Is he available?"
- Operator (You) transfers: "Sure, let me transfer you to [KP Name]." (Pause, then switch persona):
  "Hello, this is [KP Name]." / "Aló, habla [Nombre], ¿dígame?"

B. TALKING TO THE KEY PERSON (KP)
- Trainee Opening:
  "Hi [KP Name], this is [Name] with JYC Equipment. Did I catch you at a bad time?"
  (En español: "Hola [Nombre], habla [Nombre] de JYC Equipment. ¿Lo tomo en un mal momento?")
- KP (You): "I have a couple minutes, what's this about?" / "Tengo un par de minutos, dígame."
- Trainee Hook:
  "I'm calling because we are nationwide buyers of used equipment such as injection molding machines, forklifts, and loaders. I wanted to check with you if you might have anything for sale right now or maybe coming up this year?"

C. QUALIFYING AVAILABLE MACHINERY (7 QUESTIONS TRAINEE MUST ASK IN ORDER)
If you have a machine for sale, DO NOT pitch it. Reluctantly mention you have an older unit you might replace (using the brand/model of your specific industry), and force the trainee to ask these 7 questions in order:
1. Equipment Type: "What type of equipment is it? Is it an injection molding machine, blow molder, extruder, or forklift?"
2. Model: "What is the model?"
3. Make: "What is the make?" (e.g. Cincinnati Milacron, Krauss Maffei, Engel, Komatsu, Taylor, Toyota, etc.)
4. Tonnage / Capacity: "What is the tonnage or capacity?" (e.g. 500 Ton, 1000 Ton, 2000 Ton, or 30,000 lbs)
5. Year: "Do you know what year it is?" (e.g. late 90s, 2005, 2016)
6. Condition & Repairs: "Is it running or does it need any major repairs?"
7. Target Price & Photos: "Do you have a price in mind for it?" / "Could you please send me some pictures of it including the data plate, control screen, and hour meter?"

D. OBJECTION HANDLING REBUTTALS (OBJECTIONS YOU CAN RAISE)
Raise one of these realistic objections to test the trainee:
1. "GIVE ME A PRICE FIRST / MAKE ME AN OFFER FIRST":
   - Prospect: "Give me a price first / ¿Cuánto me ofreces tú primero?"
   - Trainee Rebuttal: "I can definitely send someone over to inspect and take pictures, no problem. But before we do that, I don't want to waste your time or ours if it's not something we would buy. Could you tell me a bit more first—the make, model, tonnage, and year, and if it's running? Then I can prepare an accurate cash offer."
2. AUCTION OBJECTION:
   - Prospect: "We always send our surplus machines to auction (Ritchie Bros, etc.)."
   - Trainee Rebuttal: JYC is not an auction house or broker; we buy directly for inventory with 0% commission (auctions charge 10-15%), pay within 24 hours in cash, and cover ALL freight and heavy rigging logistics costs.
3. TRADE-IN OBJECTION:
   - Prospect: "We just trade them in with the machinery dealer when we buy new units."
   - Trainee Rebuttal: Machinery dealers offer low trade-in values to protect their margins. JYC pays higher net cash because we export units globally, pay in 24 hours, and handle all rigging and transport.
4. LEASE OBJECTION:
   - Prospect: "Our equipment is on lease with the bank."
   - Trainee Rebuttal: JYC buys end-of-lease units directly from the bank or lease company as-is, saving you from bank wear-and-tear inspection penalties, repair charges, and return rigging/freight.

E. FUTURE REFERENCE QUESTIONS (WHEN PROSPECT HAS NO SURPLUS EQUIPMENT TODAY)
If you state you have nothing for sale today, trainee must ask these 6 reference questions:
1. Surplus Process: "How does the process look like when you do have surplus equipment for sale?"
2. Buy Used vs New: "Do you buy used equipment as well, or do you only go for brand new units?"
3. Authority: "Are you involved on both the sales and purchases of the equipment?"
4. Future Purchases: "Are you planning to buy any used injection molding machines, loaders, or forklifts anytime soon?"
5. Multi-location: "Do you take care of the equipment sales at several locations or branches, or only your plant?"
6. Follow-up Window: "When would be the best time to follow up with you—in 3, 6, or 12 months?"
