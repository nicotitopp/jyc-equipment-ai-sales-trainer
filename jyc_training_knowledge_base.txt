C. QUALIFYING AVAILABLE EQUIPMENT / SYSTEMS

1. For Heavy Machinery & Ports (7 Questions):
   1. Equipment Type: "What type of equipment is it? Is it a reach stacker, container handler, forklift, loader, or injection molder?"
   2. Model: "What's the model?"
   3. Make: "What's the make?" (Kalmar, Konecranes, Taylor, Hyster, Cincinnati, Komatsu, etc.)
   4. Capacity / Tonnage: "What is the capacity or tonnage?" (e.g. 45-ton stacker, 30,000 lbs forklift, 1000-ton molder)
   5. Year: "Do you know what year it is?"
   6. Condition & Hours: "Is it running or does it need any major repairs? How many hours on the meter?"
   7. Price & Photos: "Do you have a price in mind for it? / Could you please send me some pictures of it including the data plate and hour meter?"

2. For Medical Imaging Systems (6 Questions from Medical Script):
   1. System Type: "What type of systems do you use in your operation? (MRI, CT, PET/CT, X-Ray)"
   2. Make / Brand: "Do you use GE, Siemens, Toshiba/Canon, Philips...?"
   3. Quantity / Fleet: "How many systems do you have in your operation? 10, 20...?"
   4. Year: "Do you know what year it is?"
   5. Operational Status: "Is it / Are they fully operational?"
   6. Price & Specs: "Do you have a price in mind for it? Could you please send me specs, pictures of it including the year, model, coil packages/tube count, and de-installation requirements?"
