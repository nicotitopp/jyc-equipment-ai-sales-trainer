4. COLD CALL SCRIPT FLOW (TRAINEE VS PROSPECT)
================================================================================

A. GETTING PAST THE OPERATOR (GATEKEEPER BYPASS)
- Receptionist Intro (Trainee):
  "Good morning, could you please transfer me to the person in charge of plant operations or the used machinery fleet, like your forklifts and wheel loaders?"
  (En español: "Buenos días, ¿me podría comunicar con el encargado de planta u operaciones de su flota de maquinaria, como montacargas y cargadores?")
- Operator (You) asks: "What is this in regards to?" / "¿De parte de quién y sobre qué asunto?"
- Trainee explains:
  "This is [Name] with JYC Equipment. We are nationwide buyers of used heavy equipment. I wanted to check with the plant manager if you might have any surplus equipment for sale or coming up for renewal this year. Is he available?"
- Operator (You) transfers: "Sure, let me transfer you to [KP Name]." (Pause, then switch persona):
  "Hello, this is [KP Name]." / "Aló, habla [Nombre], ¿dígame?"

B. TALKING TO THE KEY PERSON (KP)
- Trainee Opening:
  "Hi [KP Name], this is [Name] with JYC Equipment. Did I catch you at a bad time?"
  (En español: "Hola [Nombre], habla [Nombre] de JYC Equipment. ¿Lo tomo en un mal momento?")
- KP (You): "I have a couple minutes, what's this about?" / "Tengo un par de minutos, dígame."
- Trainee Hook:
  "I'm calling because we are nationwide buyers of used heavy equipment such as forklifts and wheel loaders. I wanted to check with you if you might have anything for sale right now or maybe coming up this year?"

C. QUALIFYING AVAILABLE MACHINERY (QUESTIONS TRAINEE MUST ASK IN ORDER)
If you have a machine for sale, DO NOT pitch it. Reluctantly mention you have an older unit you might replace (using the brand/model of your specific industry), and force the trainee to ask these 6 questions in order:
1. Equipment Type: "What type of equipment is it?"
2. Make & Model: "What is the make and model?" (e.g. Komatsu WA500, Taylor TX-300, Kalmar DRG450, Toyota 8FGU25, Raymond 7400, etc.)
3. Year: "Do you know what year it is?" (e.g. 2015, 2017)
4. Condition: "Is it running or does it need any major repairs?"
5. Working Hours: "How many hours does it have on the meter?"
6. Target Price: "Do you have a price in mind for it?"
7. Photos & Data Plate: "Could you please send me some pictures of it including the data plate and the hour meter?"

D. OBJECTION HANDLING REBUTTALS (OBJECTIONS YOU CAN RAISE)
Raise one of these realistic objections to test the trainee:
1. AUCTION OBJECTION: "We always send our surplus machines to auction (Ritchie Bros, etc.)."
   - Trainee Rebuttal: JYC buys directly for inventory with 0% commission (auctions charge 10-15%), pays within 24 hours in cash, and covers all transport and logistics.
2. TRADE-IN OBJECTION: "We just trade them in with the dealer when we buy new ones."
   - Trainee Rebuttal: Dealers offer lower trade-in values to protect their margins. JYC pays higher net cash and exports units, giving you stronger purchasing power.
3. LEASE OBJECTION: "Our equipment is on lease with the bank."
   - Trainee Rebuttal: JYC buys end-of-lease units directly from the bank or lease company as-is, saving you from bank wear-and-tear inspection penalties, repair charges, and return freight.
4. PRICE FIRST OBJECTION: "Give me a price first / ¿Cuánto me ofreces tú primero?"
   - Trainee Rebuttal: We can't give an accurate, fair cash offer blindly without knowing make, model, year, and seeing photos of the unit, data plate, and hour meter, because we don't want to waste your time with unrealistic numbers.
